Find the place. Plan the trip.

A working prototype of the Vaya travel app. Everything in the phone can be tapped.

  • Tap any destination to open it. The picture grows into the page.
  • Tap a heart to save a place, then find it under Favorites.
  • Press Plan trip to pick a month, nights and travelers.
  • In Explore, search, pick a category, or sort and filter.
  • Tap any rating to read the reviews for that place.
  • Log out from Profile to try log in, sign up, Apple or Google sign-in and guest mode.
